# Upwork / Toptal Proposal 写作指南

> 目标：第8周开始真实投递，累计投递10+份，获得至少1次客户回复。

## 📝 Proposal 模板

```
Hi [Client Name],

I read your project description carefully and I'm confident I can
deliver exactly what you need.

**Understanding your needs**
[One paragraph showing you understand the project - mention specific
requirements from their job post to prove you actually read it]

**My approach**
- [Key technology/approach 1 relevant to their project]
- [Key technology/approach 2]
- [Key technology/approach 3]

**Relevant experience**
I recently completed [similar project description] where I [specific
achievement with numbers]. This experience directly applies to your
project because [connection to their needs].

**Timeline & Pricing**
- Estimated duration: [X] weeks
- Rate: $[X]/hour (or fixed price: $[X])
- Available to start: [e.g., immediately / next Monday]

**What you get working with me**
- Clear communication and regular updates
- Clean, well-documented code
- [X] days of free post-delivery support

I'd love to discuss your project in more detail. Are you available
for a quick call today or tomorrow?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Link to portfolio/GitHub/LinkedIn]
```

## 🎯 高分 Proposal 要点

1. **个性化开头**：提到客户项目描述中的具体细节，证明你读了
2. **展示理解**：用自己的话复述需求，显示你理解了
3. **相关经验**：只提与当前项目相关的经验，用数据量化成果
4. **具体方案**：给出你打算怎么做的简要技术方案
5. **明确报价**：给出清晰的时间和价格，不要模糊
6. **Call to Action**：结尾邀请客户进一步沟通

## ❌ 常见错误

- ❌ 复制粘贴通用模板（客户一眼就能看出来）
- ❌ 只说"我有经验"，不说具体是什么经验
- ❌ 报价模糊（"depends on requirements"）
- ❌ 太长（超过300词客户不会读完）
- ❌ 语法错误（用 Grammarly 检查）
