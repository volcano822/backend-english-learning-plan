# 邮件模板库

| 场景 | 关键句型/表达 | 完整模板要点 | 完成状态 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 初次接触/自我介绍 | I'm a full-stack developer with 10+ years of experience...<br>I specialize in...<br>I'd be happy to discuss your project. | 1.一句话定位<br>2.核心技能+年限<br>3.相关项目经验(2-3个)<br>4.表达合作意愿<br>5.Call to Action | 未完成 |
| 报价/Proposal | Based on your requirements, I estimate...<br>My rate is $X/hour<br>The project will take approximately X weeks | 1.理解需求(复述确认)<br>2.技术方案简述<br>3.时间+报价<br>4.交付物清单<br>5.为什么选我(差异化) | 未完成 |
| 需求确认 | To make sure I understand correctly...<br>Could you please clarify...<br>Here's my understanding of the scope: | 1.复述需求要点<br>2.列出待确认问题(编号)<br>3.提出假设<br>4.确认后再开工 | 未完成 |
| 进度更新 | Here's a quick update on the project...<br>Completed: ...<br>In progress: ...<br>Next steps: ... | 1.当前进度百分比<br>2.已完成事项<br>3.进行中事项<br>4.下一步计划<br>5.是否有风险/延期 | 未完成 |
| 催款 | I hope you're doing well.<br>I'm following up on the invoice #XXX sent on DATE.<br>Please let me know if there are any issues. | 1.友好开场<br>2.明确发票编号和金额<br>3.提醒付款期限<br>4.询问是否有问题<br>5.提供付款方式 | 未完成 |
| 项目交付 | I'm pleased to inform you that the project is complete.<br>Here's what was delivered: ...<br>Please review and let me know if any adjustments are needed. | 1.宣布完成<br>2.交付物清单<br>3.使用说明/文档链接<br>4.请求验收<br>5.后续支持说明 | 未完成 |
| 延期说明 | I'm writing to inform you about a delay in the project...<br>The new estimated completion date is...<br>Here's what happened and how I'm addressing it. | 1.直接说明延期<br>2.原因(诚实但简洁)<br>3.新的交付时间<br>4.已采取的措施<br>5.道歉+承诺 | 未完成 |
| 范围变更 | I've reviewed the new requirements you mentioned.<br>This is outside the original scope, but I can accommodate it.<br>The additional work would cost $X and take X days. | 1.确认收到新需求<br>2.说明是否在原范围内<br>3.额外工作量评估<br>4.追加报价和时间<br>5.等待确认后执行 | 未完成 |
| 砍价回应 | I understand your budget constraints.<br>Let me explain why my rate is set at this level...<br>I can offer X alternative to fit your budget. | 1.表示理解<br>2.解释定价依据(价值/经验)<br>3.提供替代方案(缩小范围/分期)<br>4.不直接降价，降范围<br>5.保持专业 | 未完成 |
| 拒绝不合理需求 | I appreciate you bringing this up.<br>However, this request would require...<br>I'd recommend an alternative approach: ... | 1.感谢反馈<br>2.说明为什么不可行(技术/时间)<br>3.提供替代方案<br>4.解释替代方案的好处<br>5.保持合作态度 | 未完成 |
